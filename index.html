Step-by-step rollout

From one barangay to the whole region

Each phase builds toward a connected ecosystem — combining technology, compassion, and local collaboration for sustainable growth.

Phase 1

Pilot Launch

3–6 Months · Poblacion Margosatubig
  • ✓Deploy a small fleet of motorcycles for passenger transport and light deliveries.
  • ✓Introduce the “Basket Service” for wet & dry goods via phone, Facebook, or chat.
  • ✓Launch the “Perpetual HelpLine” for bookings, inquiries, and assistance.
  • ✓Riders serve dual roles — delivery and motor-hailing — for efficiency.
Phase 2

Expansion

6–12 Months · Nearby Towns
  • ✓Separate riders into specialized teams — Motor Hailing and Goods Delivery.
  • ✓Formalize supplier partnerships with markets, farms, and pharmacies.
  • ✓Launch a Facebook page and simple ordering system (Google Forms or chatbot).
  • ✓Offer parish discounts, student fares, and loyalty cards.
Phase 3

Tech Integration

12–18 Months · Mobile App
  • ✓Develop a mobile app (Flutter + Firebase) for booking, ordering, and tracking.
  • ✓Enable digital payments via GCash, PayMaya, and bank transfer.
  • ✓Implement GPS fleet management and maintenance logs.
  • ✓Introduce customer rewards and points system.
Phase 4

Regional Growth

18–36 Months · Multi-Town
  • ✓Expand coverage to nearby municipalities.
  • ✓Launch a franchise / partnership model under the Perpetual HelpLine brand.
  • ✓Secure corporate accounts with schools, hospitals, and offices.
  • ✓Run faith-anchored campaigns — “Driven by Care, Connected by Faith.”

Team duties & functions

The people behind every ride

A lean, faith-anchored organization built to deliver safe, reliable, and compassionate service.

⚙ Operations Manager

Oversees all logistics, ride-hailing services, and delivery schedules. Coordinates fleet, riders, and customer care operations. Ensures service quality and operational efficiency. Monitors performance metrics and implements service improvements. Provides vision, strategy, and faith-anchored leadership.

🏍 Fleet & Riders

Provides safe, reliable, and courteous service. Maintains vehicles and adheres to safety standards. Represents the company’s values in every ride and delivery. Ensures punctuality and professionalism. Acts as frontline ambassadors of compassionate mobility.

🎧 Customer Care

Handles hotline and chat support for orders and concerns. Resolves customer issues with empathy and professionalism. Collects feedback to enhance customer experience and strengthen trust. Maintains records of customer interactions for quality assurance.

📣 Marketing & Partnerships

Builds brand awareness through campaigns, promotions, collaborations, and community engagement. Establishes partnerships with suppliers, local businesses, and institutions. Develops strategies to expand reach and strengthen market presence.

🛡 Motor Hailing Supervisor

Manages rider schedules, routes, and daily operations. Ensures safety compliance and traffic regulation adherence. Conducts rider briefings and performance evaluations. Coordinates with Dispatch and Customer Care for smooth service flow. Handles incident reporting and corrective actions.

🛵 Motor Hailing Riders

Provide safe, courteous passenger transport. Deliver light parcels promptly and securely. Maintain vehicle cleanliness and professional conduct. Report route issues and maintenance needs. Represent Perpetual HelpLine’s values of care and reliability.

📞 Dispatch Coordinator

Assigns rides and deliveries via hotline, chat, or app. Monitors rider availability and optimizes dispatch efficiency. Tracks ongoing trips and ensures timely completion. Communicates updates to customers and resolves scheduling conflicts. Maintains dispatch logs and coordinates with the Supervisor.

🧮 Finance & Administration

Manages billing, payroll, and compliance. Oversees budgeting, reporting, and resource allocation. Ensures transparency and accountability across operations. Supports administrative needs for smooth organizational functioning.
